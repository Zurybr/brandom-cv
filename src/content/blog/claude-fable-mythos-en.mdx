A migration across 50 million lines of Ruby, quoted at two months for a full engineering team, finished in a day. Protein design workflows accelerated ten-fold. And the strangest part: both feats come from the *same brain* wearing two different names.

On June 9, 2026, Anthropic launched **Claude Fable 5** and **Claude Mythos 5** — one architecture, two models, separated only by their safeguards. As an engineer running language models in production, I think this is the most consequential release design we have seen. Here is why.

## Two Models, One Architecture

**Fable 5** is a Mythos-class model made safe for general use. **Mythos 5** is the same model with safeguards lifted in specific areas, available only to accredited cyber defenders and researchers.

> *Fable* comes from the Latin *fabula*, "that which is told," akin to the Greek *mythos*. The safeguards are what distinguish the two models — and why they have different names.

![Benchmark comparison](/images/blog/claude-fable-mythos/infographic.png)

## Technical Capabilities

### Software Engineering

Stripe reported that Fable 5 compressed months of engineering into days. In a 50-million-line Ruby codebase, the model completed a codebase-wide migration in a day that would have taken a full team over two months by hand.

![Agents and tools](/images/blog/claude-fable-mythos/agents-tools.png)

On Cognition's FrontierCode evaluation, which tests whether models can pass difficult coding tasks while meeting production codebase standards, Fable 5 scores highest among frontier models — even at medium effort. This is remarkable: the model achieves better code quality with fewer tokens than its predecessors.

### Knowledge Work

On Hebbia's Finance Benchmark for senior-level reasoning, Fable 5 has the highest score of any model. IMC noted that it aced their trading-analysis evaluations nearly across the board: factual lookup, conceptual reasoning, root-cause analysis, and expected-value analysis.

### Vision

Fable 5 is the new state-of-the-art model for tasks involving vision. It can extract precise numbers from detailed scientific figures and rebuild a web app's source code from screenshots alone. But the most striking demonstration: it beat Pokémon FireRed using only raw game screenshots — no maps, navigation aids, or extra game-state information. Previous Claude models needed a complex helper harness; Fable did it with vision alone.

### Memory and Long Context

Fable 5 stays focused across millions of tokens in long-running tasks. When playing *Slay the Spire*, giving it access to persistent file-based memory improved its performance three times more than it did for Opus 4.8 — and Fable reached the game's final act three times more often.

## Scientific Research with Mythos 5

### Drug Design

Anthropic's internal protein design experts accelerated aspects of the drug design process by around ten times using Mythos 5. In one example, Mythos 5 — with protein design and bioinformatics tools but no human assistance — matched or beat skilled human operators, executing all tasks normally completed by a scientist: choosing binding sites, selecting and running protein design tools, and recovering from failures.

![Protein design by Mythos 5](/images/blog/claude-fable-mythos/fable-writing.png)

Nine of the 14 protein targets from this study yielded strong candidates for drug design that Anthropic is currently investigating.

### Novel Hypotheses in Molecular Biology

Mythos 5 is Anthropic's first model to consistently produce novel, compelling scientific hypotheses. In blinded head-to-head comparisons against Opus-class models, scientists preferred Mythos's molecular biology hypotheses ~80% of the time. One Mythos hypothesis — a novel mechanism for an *E. coli* protein — was independently corroborated by a lab working on the same problem.

### Genomics Research

Mythos 5 conducted novel genomics research over a week of largely autonomous work. It assembled single-cell data for millions of cells spanning 138 animal species and designed a custom machine learning model that outperformed a recent model published in *Science* — despite being 100 times smaller.

## The Safeguard System

Releasing a model with these capabilities carries real risks. Without safeguards, Fable 5's cybersecurity capabilities could be misused to cause serious damage.

![Safeguard architecture](/images/blog/claude-fable-mythos/fable-architecture.png)

### Safety Classifiers

When Fable's classifiers detect a request related to cybersecurity, biology/chemistry, or distillation, the response is automatically handled by **Claude Opus 4.8** instead. Users are informed whenever this occurs.

Over **95% of Fable sessions** involve no fallback at all. For those sessions, Fable 5's performance is effectively the same as Mythos 5.

### Red Teaming

Anthropic subjected their classifiers to extensive red teaming:

- An external bug bounty produced **zero universal jailbreaks** in over 1,000 hours of testing
- External red-teaming organizations also failed to find universal jailbreaks on long-form agentic tasks
- One external partner found that Fable 5 had **zero compliances** with harmful cybersecurity requests, even across 30 public jailbreak techniques

![Cybersecurity results](/images/blog/claude-fable-mythos/benchmark-results.png)

### Data Retention

For Mythos-class models, Anthropic requires 30-day data retention for all business customers. The data is **not used to train new Claude models** and is deleted after 30 days in almost all cases. Harvey, one of Anthropic's legal partners, flagged this data processing difference as an important consideration for their clients.

## Early Adoption

Early testers report impressive results:

- **Cursor**: "State of the art on CursorBench. Opened up a class of long-horizon problems out of reach for earlier models."
- **GitHub**: "Took on complex, long-horizon coding tasks with autonomy and reliability that exceeded previous benchmarks."
- **Stripe**: "Compressed months of engineering into days."
- **Harvey**: On the Legal Agent Benchmark (LAB), Fable 5 scored **13.3%** — an all-time high, up from Opus 4.8's 10.4%. On BigLaw Bench, it reached **93.4%**.

![Model comparison](/images/blog/claude-fable-mythos/model-comparison.png)

## Pricing and Availability

- **$10 per million input tokens**, **$50 per million output tokens** — less than half the price of Claude Mythos Preview
- Available via API as `claude-fable-5`
- Included on Pro/Max/Team plans through June 22, then requires usage credits until capacity allows standard availability

## What This Means

As a Principal AI Engineer, I see three key implications:

1. **Long-horizon autonomous agents are now viable**: Fable 5 maintains coherence across millions of tokens. This fundamentally changes what tasks you can delegate to an agent.

2. **The safety-capability gap is closing**: Anthropic demonstrates it's possible to release Mythos-class models with robust safeguards. It's not perfect — false positives exist — but it's a significant step forward.

3. **Pricing trends toward democratization**: At less than half the price of Mythos Preview, these capabilities are reaching more developers and researchers.

The coming months will be telling. The expansion of the trusted access program for biology and cybersecurity, along with continuous classifier improvement, will determine whether this balanced release model proves sustainable.
